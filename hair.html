THE ART OF THE CUT

Good shape changes
the whole feeling.

The best haircut isn’t only beautiful when you leave the salon. It makes sense when you wake up, when you style it yourself and when life gets busy.

That’s why a thoughtful cut starts with your natural texture, face-framing preferences, lifestyle and the amount of styling you enjoy. A little conversation can help turn an inspiration photo into something personal.

For a subtle refreshTrim, tidy and restore the shape you already love.

For a change of paceDiscuss length, layers, fringe or a new silhouette.

For everyday polishAsk about a finish that complements your routine.

AT-HOME NOTES

Make the salon
feeling last.

Small habits can help you enjoy your look for longer. Ask which products and styling steps make sense for your hair rather than buying a full shelf of things you may not need.

  • Choose a routine that suits your texture and wash schedule.
  • Use heat thoughtfully and protect hair when styling.
  • Follow personalized advice for your cut or color.
  • Book maintenance when your shape or color needs it.
