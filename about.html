THE HEART OF CROSS PLAINS

Come as you are.
Leave feeling refreshed.

A good salon visit should feel like time well spent: a friendly welcome, a thoughtful conversation and a look that makes sense for you.

Cross Plains Beauty Salon is part of the everyday rhythm of this community. It’s a place for familiar faces, a fresh haircut, a new idea or simply a moment to take care of yourself.

Our approach is rooted in personal attention. We want to understand what you like, what your hair naturally does and what you want your look to say—then work toward a result that feels comfortable and authentic.
