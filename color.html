YOUR COLOR CHECKLIST

Before the first
brushstroke.

Arrive ready to talk about your hair history and your goals. Clear information helps your stylist guide you toward a more considered result.

  • Share previous color, lightening or chemical treatments.
  • Bring a few inspiration photos in natural-looking light.
  • Discuss how often you realistically want to maintain it.
  • Ask about timing and pricing before confirming.
COLOR QUESTIONS

Let’s clear a few things up.

Can I go much lighter in one visit? +

It depends on your starting color, previous treatments and hair condition. A consultation helps establish a realistic, hair-conscious plan.

How often will I need maintenance? +

That varies by technique, contrast, hair growth and your preferred look. Discuss a maintenance rhythm before deciding.

Can I get a quote before booking? +

Yes—contact the salon and share your current hair, desired result and any relevant color history so you can ask about pricing and timing.
